Research scope

Visual contribution

The system coordinates cohort distributions, full movement paths, decision-index evidence, candidate identities, and geometric alternatives. It distinguishes missing observations from model uncertainty and preserves global action identity even when local candidates are incomplete.

Evidence limits

Cohorts depend on features and initialization. Geometry does not establish instruction compliance, and observed associations do not establish causation. The cases are retrospective trace analyses; no expert interview or controlled user study is claimed.

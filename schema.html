Trace schema

Supported files

Original JSON and JSONL traces and shared-topology compact JSON are accepted. Episode identities combine dataset, model, split, scan, and episode ID. Duplicate identities retain the first record.

Evidence fields

Preserve full viewpoint IDs, chosen action, raw candidate probabilities, complete movement segments, graph coordinates, instruction, and outcome validity. Candidate bearings do not uniquely identify actions. Missing probabilities must remain missing. Test outcomes are disabled unless explicitly valid.

See TRACE_SCHEMA.md for field-level documentation inherited from the compatible 3.0 data format.
